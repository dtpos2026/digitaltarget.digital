09 · Rules
Rules that keep every product one product

  1. One logo mark (the DT mark). Apps add a small role badge in a corner of the icon (bike = rider, crown = owner); screens never restyle the mark.
  2. The restaurant's own logo and name lead in customer-facing screens; "Powered by Digital Target" is the small line at the foot.
  3. A screen shows only what the account can do: no greyed teasers for modules that are off or not permitted.
  4. Anything that does not exist in the backend yet is drawn with the dashed "needs backend" tag and listed in 09-handoff — never faked as working.
  5. Money is always the restaurant's currency symbol from settings, never a hard-coded "PKR" (the design uses Rs only because the sample restaurant is in Pakistan).
  6. Every list has an empty, a loading and an error state, in that shape (see SCREENS.md of each product).
  7. Dark theme is first-class on phones and the kitchen screen; contrast numbers above are measured for both.
