Design package build order, **step 4 (phone apps inner screens)**, third part: the rest of the Owner app (`design-project/06-owner-app`). The Rider app was v1.88.0, the Order Taker app v1.89.0.

Until now, these rows of the Owner app's **More** tab opened the till's full pages on the owner's phone. They are now phone screens, in the Owner app's look. Each one appears only for an account whose role may open that module, as before.

### Approving from a phone — a problem found and solved before it shipped
Approving an order queues its kitchen ticket **on the device that approves**, and marks the order "ticket printed".
- At the till that is right.
- On the owner's phone, which has no printer, the ticket would never print, yet the order would say it had. The till prints automatically only brand-new orders, so the kitchen would only have seen it on the kitchen screen.
- Cross-device printing ("cloud print") exists only for the old Firebase backend. It is switched off on the current one.

So an approval from the Owner app works differently:
1. It sets the order running **without** claiming the ticket, and marks it `kotFromTill`.
2. The till's new-order watcher prints that ticket through **the same automatic-ticket rule it uses for website orders**. A restaurant that sends to the kitchen by hand still does that.
3. The "ticket printed" stamp that printing writes stops a second print.
4. A till that opens more than 12 hours after the approval does not print it.

This is the only change to the till, and only an Owner-app approval can trigger it. The till's own approval and the order taker's flow are unchanged.

### Found in the browser check and fixed
- **Delivery "Waiting 288"**: delivery bills settled at the counter that never used the rider stages counted as waiting forever.
  - A settled bill with no delivery stage is now finished.
  - The board covers the last 24 hours.
  - Times read "1 h 10 min", not "70 min".
- **Credit**: bills with no customer on them were grouped under a vague "Customer". They are now their own row, "No customer on the bill".

## 4. NOT EXECUTED

- **The cloud staff list:** offline here, so the screen showed this phone's accounts and said so.
- **The DT Owner Android app itself.**
