# QAT and UAT — DT POS Enterprise v1.89.0 (DT Order Taker: the design package's order-taker screens)

Design package build order, **step 4 (phone apps inner screens)**, second part: the Order Taker app (`design-project/05b-order-taker-app`). The Rider app was the first part (v1.88.0).

## 1. What was built
The Order Taker app (`#/order-taker`, the DT Order Taker Android app) now has the design's own look: Order Taker teal on the DT neutrals, the same whatever theme the restaurant's tills use.

- **OTK-01 Start:** the teal app colour, the clipboard mark and a spinner while the saved session is read. There is no white flash.
- **OTK-02 Sign in:** the same sign-in (portal first, then the phone's own staff list when offline) in the app's look.
  - A missing field, a wrong PIN or a server lockout message is now shown **on the screen**, not only in a toast.
  - The workspace code still turns amber and says "(required)" when the username exists in two restaurants.
- **OTK-03 The app shell:**
  - **Top bar:** the tab's name, then *who · which restaurant · workspace code* (wrapping to two lines, never cut off), the location-sharing **ON / OFF** pill, and **Log out**.
  - **Tabs at the bottom, at the thumb:** POS · Tables · Bills · Me. They used to sit under the header.
  - The **POS, Tables and Bills screens are the cashier's own screens, unchanged**. They take the app's teal through the colour tokens (category chips, Add buttons, Cart, the table states).
- **OTK-12 Me:**
  - "You are taking orders for", with the restaurant's name, branch and workspace code, larger, as drawn.
  - The profile card (photo, name, phone).
  - **Share my location with my manager** (the same consent switch as the top bar) and **Dark theme**.
  - Log out; the app version; Powered by Digital Target.
- **OTK-13 The connection, said at the top of every tab:**
  - **Offline:** "Orders you take are kept on this phone and sent when you are back (N waiting)". This is true: an order saved offline goes into the phone's upload queue and is sent through the portal when the connection returns.
  - **Sending N changes…** while that queue drains.
  - **N changes could not be sent — saved on this phone**, with **Retry**: the same "stuck" changes and the same retry as the till's status bar.
- **OTK-14 Dark** for dim dining rooms. It covers the POS, Tables and Bills too, and is remembered on the phone.
- **Log out asks first** ("Log out of DT Order Taker?"), from the top bar or from Me. Before this, one stray tap on the bar's exit icon signed the waiter out. It still ends the server session (`portalLogout`).
- **Shared with DT Rider:** the phone-app kit (cards, toggles, banners, tab bar), now in the Order Taker's colours.

### Kept exactly as it was
- The portal bootstrap on the way in, which brings tables, riders and live orders.
- The offline sign-in from the phone's staff list.
- Location consent.
- The KOT printer, the auto-ready timer, the ready alerts for dine-in orders (OTK-10) and the customer's "call waiter" alerts (OTK-11).
- The routes `#/order-taker/<restaurant>/tables | bills | me`.

## 2. Not changed in this release, and why
- **OTK-05 item options, OTK-07 order review and send, OTK-09 bill detail** live inside the POS and Running Bills screens. Those are **the cashier's screens, shared with every till**. Redrawing them for the phone would change the cashier's till too. They work in the app today (the Cart sheet with Send / Kitchen and the bills list) and take the teal. A phone-only version is a separate, careful piece of work.
- **OTK-10 / OTK-11 alerts** are the existing shared banners (ready food, customer calls the waiter). They work only while the app is open: there is no push service (build order step 6).
- **Found while checking, not changed:** the Tables screen puts a button inside a button (React prints a warning). This is in the shared Tables page, not in the new shell, so it is the same on the cashier's till.
