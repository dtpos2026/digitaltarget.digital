# QAT and UAT — DT POS Enterprise v1.96.0 (Day Close page, DT Refined colour schemes, design-gap audit)

Prompted by the owner's review of v1.93.0. Three things came out of it; the third is a document, not code.

## 1. What was built
### Day Close has its own page (`#/day-close`, sidebar → Day Close)
It was a tab at the bottom of Settings. The page (the offline POS's structure, with this app's own safe logic) goes in order:
1. **Shift report** — today, yesterday, last 7 days, this month, this year or custom dates, on the thermal printer; the drawer float.
2. **What Day Close clears** (admin) — paid, running/HOLD, void/complimentary, credit; tables to Free; restart the daily order number; JSON backup first; and the **modules to reset to 00**. Ticks are saved at once and remembered.
3. **Close the day** — the confirmation lists exactly how many bills leave the live lists, per group, how many stay, and warns about unpaid bills that will be removed from the running list. A cashier with the permission can only **request**; the admin confirms.
The work itself is unchanged from Settings (v1.27.1 and later): it stops while bills have not reached the server, archives instead of deleting, waits for the server before clearing anything on the till, resets the order counter on the cloud, reports what the server confirmed, and writes the audit entry. The classification is now one function (`planDayClose`) used both for the numbers on screen and for the work, so what is shown is what is done. The Settings tab now only points to the page; the old copy of the workflow is removed from Settings.

### DT Refined colour schemes (Settings → Appearance → DT Refined → Colour scheme)
DT Refined had one fixed look. It now offers **seven ready-made schemes** (DT Purple, Ocean, Emerald, Sunset, Rose, Graphite, Night), **five page colours** (lavender, clean grey, warm cream, cool mist, night) and the restaurant's **own accent colour** (the same chooser Modern has). Night makes the whole page and panels dark. The Refined shell (sidebar, top bar, cards, active item, focus ring) now follows the colour tokens instead of a fixed white and purple. Presentation only: one stored preference and one attribute on `<html>`; the other themes are unaffected.

### The audit — `docs/DESIGN-GAP-AUDIT-2026-11-02.md`
A screen-by-screen account of what was built from the design package and what was not, with the POS side by side. **1 of 40 restaurant-software screens is built to the package's layout (the Dashboard)**; v1.85.0 delivered the shell and tokens, not the inner screens. It also records the plan, in the owner's order: customer website first, then a component pass, then the POS, then the rest, then one brand identity and the alignment sweep.

## 2. NOT changed, and why
- **The POS screen, Tables, Retrieve, Delivery, Pickup, Kitchen and the management screens keep their older layout.** That is the polish programme in the audit, after the customer website, as the owner asked. They do follow the chosen colour scheme.
- **"Reset Sale"** (the offline Day Close page's second tab, which zeroes selected modules immediately) is not added: the modules are part of step 2 here, and Backup & Restore → Erase Selected Data does the immediate reset with the cloud-correct result reporting.
- The sign-in screen has no colour scheme (it has its own look).
