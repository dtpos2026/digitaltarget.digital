# QAT and UAT — DT POS Enterprise v1.85.0 (DT design package, step 1: DT Refined theme, shell and SW-02 dashboard)

Requested by the owner: build the DT design package (`design-project/`, DT-POS-Design-Package-v1.0). Built in the package's own order (`design-project/09-handoff/BUILD-ORDER.md`). Step 1 is the Windows/web inner shell (sidebar, top bar) and the SW-02 dashboard. It ships as a new theme, **DT Refined**, so every existing theme, route and permission keeps working.

## 1. What was built
- **DT Refined theme** (Settings → Appearance, first choice). Its colours are the design tokens (`01-design-system/tokens.css`): accent `#5b21b6`, page `#f7f5fb`, white surfaces, border `#e4def0`, text `#1a1523`, muted `#6b6478`, tint `#f3effc`. Cards have a 16 px radius, the top bar is 64 px, the sidebar is 248 px (72 collapsed), and focus shows a 3 px ring in the accent at 25 %. Every rule in `ui-refined.css` is scoped to this theme. The theme is **open to every restaurant**: it is not part of the premium set, and the premium gate leaves it alone. Every other theme is unchanged.
- **Sidebar** (SW-02 / SW-10): white. Favourites come first, then the design's groups in order: Operations, Marketing, Inventory, Accounts, Staff, Reports, Admin.
  - A closed group is a row with its icon, the number of modules in it, and a chevron. An open group is a small label above its modules.
  - The current page's group opens by itself, and the app remembers which groups were left open.
  - The current page is a filled accent pill. Only one item is filled, even where two entries share a route (Day Close opens Settings). The current page is scrolled into view.
  - The footer has Settings, a status card, the user and log out. The status card says Online/Offline and the sync queue (empty / N changes waiting / sync needs attention). It never claims "synced" when that is not true.
  - Who sees what is unchanged: role permissions, the plan and hidden modules all still apply.
- **Top bar:**
  - A wide "Search modules…" box from 1280 px up (an icon below that).
  - A **Status** popover holding the connection, print-mode, setup, version and billing chips. They are one click away instead of a row of chips.
  - The bell, the sync chip, the clock, text size, and a user chip (initials, name, role, log out). The second small "Synced" label is not repeated.
- **SW-02 dashboard.** It is shown with DT Refined; the standard dashboard is one click away, and other themes keep the v1.80.0 Overview.
  - The greeting shows restaurant · branch · weekday. Above the cards: **Today / Week / Month** and **Export** (CSV).
  - **Sales** card: Today is compared with **the same weekday last week, up to the same clock time**. Week and Month are compared with the period before.
  - **Orders** card: "n more / fewer than …". **Average bill** card: % change. **Open bills** card: open right now, split by order type.
  - **Sales — last 7 days** chart (last 30 days on Month).
  - **Payment mix**. A split bill is divided by its payments. Credit is its own slice, with a note that it enters Sales when collected.
  - **Top items** by quantity, and **Orders by hour** (the current hour on Today, the busiest hour otherwise).
  - **Needs attention**: online orders awaiting approval, bills left open from an earlier business day, low stock and credit owed. Each one opens the page that fixes it.
  - **Recent orders**.
  - Every figure is computed in `lib/refinedDashboard.ts` with the same "paid sale" rule as the reports. The dashboard refreshes when orders or stock change (at most every 3 s) and every minute.
- **Fix found during the browser check — auto-ready kitchen timer.**
  - The timer counted any bill that was not void/cancelled and had no "ready"/"served" kitchen status as still cooking. That included paid bills settled outside the POS (the POS stamps "served"), online orders still awaiting approval, and bills from last week.
  - Five seconds after start-up it therefore re-saved every such bill and announced "READY" for it, each with a beep and a toast. With ~600 seeded bills, Chromium froze.
  - Now only open bills count, plus settled bills the kitchen is still working on (pay-first takeaway), and only within 12 hours. The check lives in `lib/autoReady.ts`.

## 2. Differences from the design, on purpose
| Design (SW-02) | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Sample data" badge on the sales chart | not shown | Every figure is real; nothing is sample data. |
| "Synced 12 s ago" | "Synced" chip + the sidebar status card | The store reports the sync state, not the time of the last sync; a time would be invented. |
| "Day close for yesterday is not done" | "N bills from an earlier day are still open" | Day-close records are in the cloud table `day_closes`; reading them on every dashboard load would add egress. Bills left open from an earlier business day are visible locally, and they are what a day close catches. |
| "9 more than avg" | "N more than last Tue" | The same comparison as the Sales card, so the two cards agree. |
| Branch selector in the top bar | Branch named in the greeting; switching is where it was | Step 1 does not change how branches are switched. |
| No clock / text size in the top bar | Kept | Cashiers use them; nothing was removed. |
| Sidebar collapses to icons at 1024 px | Collapse stays the existing toggle | Unchanged behaviour; the layout was checked at 1024 px. |
