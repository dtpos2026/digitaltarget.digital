# QAT and UAT — DT POS Enterprise v1.81.0 (the Espresso Orange / DT Retail order screen in the cloud POS; floor and dashboard aligned to the design package)

## Why
The owner showed the **offline** POS in DT Retail → Espresso Orange (sign-in and order screen) and said the online one still has the simple old order screen; and that new work must follow **our own design package** (`design-project/`, 132 screens, DT design system).

Root cause of the POS gap (found by reading both sources): the cloud already had the themes and the stylesheet (`ui-pos.css`, 38 `data-pos-*` marks) but its `POSScreen` carried **3 of the 38 marks** and had no layout engine — so a modern theme restyled the shell and left the order screen as it was.

## 2. What changed
- **Order screen (Modern / DT Retail looks only):** the offline layout engine (`lib/posLayout.ts`, `hooks/usePosLayout.ts`) measures the POS area and decides product columns, category panel (top / side / right) and cart width; product cards, the order panel ("Order · n items"), the order-type segmented control, customer fields, cart lines with round steppers and a unit-price line, totals, payment/change, keypad, CLR / Hold / PAY and Kitchen / Customer Receipt now carry the structure the stylesheet styles.
- **Classic look untouched** by construction (tests pin it): grid classes, cart width, the ADD bar and the solid keypad stay as they were.
- **Floor plan:** tiles default to the design package's outlined card (coloured edge, time pill); "Filled" stays one click away in Customize.
- **Overview dashboard:** added the design package's "Needs attention" list (online orders awaiting approval, items below minimum, credit owed) — only what the system can see.
