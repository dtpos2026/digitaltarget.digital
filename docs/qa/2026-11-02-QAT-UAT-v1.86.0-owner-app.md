# QAT and UAT — DT POS Enterprise v1.86.0 (DT design package, step 2: the Owner app)

Requested by the owner ("Next", after step 1): continue the DT design package in its own build order (`design-project/09-handoff/BUILD-ORDER.md`). Step 2 is the Owner portal screens. OWN-04/05/06/07/11 come first, then OWN-13/14/15, all on existing data.

## 1. What was built
- **The Owner app** at `#/owner-app`, a phone layout of its own: a bottom tab bar (Overview · Orders · Menu · Reports · More). From 820 px the tab bar becomes a left rail, and the branch cards sit two per row.
  - It is rendered **outside the till's shell**. The owner's phone runs no KOT printer, no kitchen auto-ready timer and no order beeps.
  - Its look is the design tokens (DT purple, lavender page, 16 px cards), scoped to the app, whatever theme the tills use.
- **OWN-04 Overview:**
  - A greeting, then Today / Yesterday / Last 7 days / This month / This year.
  - Five figures: money taken, bills, still owed, open now, void / cancelled.
  - Shortcuts to online approvals and low stock, shown when there is something to see.
  - Every branch as a card: open-now badge, money taken, bills, still owed, share of the business (the shares add to 100), voids.
  - A branch that took nothing keeps its card. Bills without a branch appear as "No branch recorded"; a branch that no longer exists appears as "Unknown branch".
- **OWN-05 Branch detail:** sales by hour (by day for longer periods), payment split, order types, and that branch's open bills.
- **OWN-06 Orders:**
  - Status chips: All / Running / Paid / Delivery / Credit / Void. "Running" is the floor right now, whatever the date.
  - Search by number, customer, phone, table, waiter or item.
  - A filter panel for the period and the branch, "Show more" for long lists, and "Clear filters" when nothing matches.
- **OWN-07 Order detail (read-only):**
  - The status, the items, only the charges that apply, the total and the payments.
  - Customer and rider cards with Call buttons.
  - A **timeline** built only from the stamps the bill carries (placed online / opened, approved, sent to kitchen, ready, rider assigned, on the way, delivered, paid, voided with reason, cancelled, complimentary), each with who did it.
  - **Share receipt** makes a real PDF (restaurant header) and opens the phone's share sheet, or downloads it.
- **OWN-11 Reports:**
  - The period's money taken against **the same-length period before**: today against yesterday up to the same clock time, a month against the same days of last month. The change is shown in %.
  - Bars by hour, day or month, a branch picker, and top items by quantity with their amount.
  - **Export** makes a PDF.
- **OWN-13 Menu:**
  - Search, category chips, and per item an availability switch (off reads "sold out").
  - The price can be edited for fixed-price items; sized items say "Priced by size". A bad price is refused.
  - Saved exactly as the menu manager saves (`saveMenuItem`), so the POS, the ordering website and the apps follow.
- **OWN-14 Low stock:**
  - Items at or below their reorder level (the dashboard's rule), lowest first, each with a level bar.
  - Each shows **the supplier who last delivered it** (from Receiving) and a Call button with that supplier's phone (from Parties).
- **OWN-15 Expenses:**
  - The month total and expenses grouped by day.
  - An **Add expense** sheet (amount, category — or the first category typed in, note, date — not in the future, paid by), saved as the same record Accounts → Expense saves.
  - A missing amount keeps the sheet open with the reason.
  - Dates are the phone's local date. Accounts uses the UTC date, which is yesterday before 5 am in Pakistan.
- **More (OWN-19):** the profile and workspace code. Rows appear only for modules the account may open: delivery, kitchen, approvals, customers and credit, staff, expenses, inventory, website switches, devices, settings, and the full software. Sign out.
- **Who may open it:** the permission system decides. A new page key `owner-app` (owners and managers; plan **Business and up**, like Owner Management) controls the app. Each tab checks its own module (Menu → `menu`, Reports → `reports`/`owner-management`, Low stock → `inventory`, Expenses → `accounts`). A branch-limited manager sees only their branch, and never the bills without one. A cashier, or a plan without the app, is told why.
- **DT Owner Android app:**
  - `capacitor.owner.config.json` (`com.digitaltarget.dtowner`, opens `#/owner-app`).
  - Brand kit: "DT Owner" (purple, crown badge, as in OWN-01) added to `src/lib/brand.ts`, and the icon/splash generated (`branding/icons/owner.svg`; Android resources under `branding/export/android/Owner/`, generated locally as before).
  - `ANDROID_APPS.md` updated.
- **Owner Management (desktop)** is laid out as the design's desktop Overview (OWN-04 desktop) and uses the **same functions** as the phone (`lib/ownerApp.ts`), so the two cannot disagree. A **Phone view** button opens the Owner app.
- **Correction found while doing it:** Owner Management's "still owed" added the full total of every bill of the period that was not fully paid, **including bills simply open on the floor**. Those bills were already counted in "open now". "Still owed" is now what the label always said: the unpaid part of part-paid and credit bills. A paid bill owes nothing, even when an older till never wrote the amount received.

## 2. Differences from the design, on purpose
| Design | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Bell opens notifications (OWN-18) | The bell opens Online approvals, with their count | Notifications need a backend that does not exist yet (BUILD-ORDER step 6). Approvals are real and actionable now. |
| Pull to refresh | Live: the screens refresh themselves when orders, menu or stock change (at most every 1.5 s) | The data is already on the device and kept in sync; there is nothing to pull. |
| Low stock: "Open item" | The supplier and Call; adjustments stay in the full software | As the design's own note says: adjustments need the cost and supplier fields. |
| Order detail "Share receipt (PDF)" | A PDF of the bill | It is not the thermal receipt layout (that one is for printers). |
| The 5th tile on the phone | "Void / cancelled" as a full-width row under the four tiles | The specification lists five figures; the phone drawing has room for four tiles. |

## 4. NOT EXECUTED
- **The DT Owner Android app itself.** The APK must be built by you (`ANDROID_APPS.md`). Inside the Android app, **sharing a PDF** depends on the WebView: Android's WebView has no share sheet for files, so the PDF may download instead, or need the Capacitor Share plugin.
- A real restaurant's sign-in and data: several branches, a branch-limited manager against the live database rules, real sync.
- Changing a menu item from the phone while a till is open (the switch saves through the same path the menu manager uses, but two devices were not run side by side).
