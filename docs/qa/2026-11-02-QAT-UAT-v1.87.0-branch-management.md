# QAT and UAT — DT POS Enterprise v1.87.0 (Branch management, A to Z, for the owner — web and app)

Requested by the owner: "branch management system — A to Z — in the web and the app, for the owner; food business ecosystem management". This continues the multi-branch plan (`docs/MULTI-BRANCH-ARCHITECTURE.md`): Phase 1 (Branch Performance) shipped in v1.82.0, and the Owner app in v1.86.0.

## 1. What was built
- **Branch Management** (desktop, `/branch-management`, sidebar → Admin). Owners and managers; Business plan and up. Only a restaurant with two or more branches sees it.
  - **A card per branch:**
    - **Live / Quiet**: live when a till was seen in the last 5 minutes or the branch billed in the last 30. The card also shows when the last bill was.
    - Money taken, bills and open bills for the chosen period.
    - **Target progress**: today against the daily target, or this month against the pace the monthly target needs. Shows on track or behind.
    - Stock items and how many are low; **tills online / total**; stock **incoming** or **on the way out**.
  - **Branch detail tabs:**
    - **Overview**: money taken, bills, still owed, void; payment split; order types.
    - **Stock**: items, value at cost, low items first.
    - **Staff**: assigned to the branch, and those who work across all branches.
    - **Tills**: each device online now, or when it was last seen; blocked or awaiting approval.
    - **Targets**: progress, and setting the daily and monthly targets (owner).
    - **Transfers**: this branch's stock transfers.
    - **Info**: address, phone, code, invoice prefix, delivery radius, the map, and Edit in Branches.
  - **Stock between branches**: every transfer, in transit first.
- **Stock transfers** (gap G4 of the plan). New transfer: from, to, items picked from the sending branch's stock (what it has is shown next to each), quantities, a note.
  - **Send**: the stock leaves the sending branch now; the transfer is "in transit".
  - **Receive**: the receiver counts what arrived, line by line. The stock enters the receiving branch's item: the same SKU, otherwise the same name in the same unit, otherwise **created in that branch**. Its **moving-average cost** takes in the transferred units at the cost they left with. Less than was sent is recorded as a **shortfall**.
  - **Cancel** (only while in transit): the stock goes back to the sender.
  - **Never twice:** a received transfer cannot be received or cancelled again. Every stock change is one stock-ledger movement with its own id (`transfer:<id>:out|in|back:<line>`), and the ledger refuses an id it has already seen, so a retry or a replayed sync cannot move stock twice.
  - **Who may do what:** send and cancel need the sending branch; receive needs the receiving branch; moving stock needs the Inventory module. The database enforces the same rule on every stock row.
- **Owner app** (`#/owner-app`):
  - A branch now has tabs **Today · Stock · Staff · Tills · Targets · Transfers**. Today is the v1.86.0 screen.
  - **Compare branches** ranks every branch by money taken, bills, average bill, still owed or void, for any period. Still owed and void rank lowest first.
  - **Stock transfers**: the same send / receive / cancel as the desktop.
  - More has a new **Branches** section (Compare, Stock transfers, Branch management).
- **No new tables.** Transfers and targets are documents in the existing document store (`module_documents`, kinds `stockTransfers` and `branchTargets`), synced in real time like purchase orders. Transfers write through at once like other stock.
  - The staff and till lists are read from the cloud: the same RPC the Users page uses, and the devices table the Devices page uses. Both are cached for a minute.
  - Offline, these lists say they need a connection instead of looking empty.

## 2. Still to decide (owner) — the parts of "A to Z" that need you
These need a decision and an additive database change, so they are **not** built yet (`docs/MULTI-BRANCH-ARCHITECTURE.md` §5):
1. **Branch prices / availability**: may a branch set its own price, or only hide and show items? Who approves a branch change — the branch manager or the owner?
2. **Settings per branch** (receipt header, service charge, tax per branch, inheriting from the restaurant's settings).
3. **Employees, payroll, suppliers, recipes**: branch-owned, or business-wide?
4. **Customers and loyalty**: shared across branches (today), or per branch?
5. **Brands**: one owner with several restaurant names above the branches?
