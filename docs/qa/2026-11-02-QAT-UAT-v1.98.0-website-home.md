# QAT and UAT — v1.98.0 (customer website — new home, design package WEB-01)

Step 3 of the build order, part 2. On top of v1.95.0 (the website knows the owner's switches). No data or database change: every rule of ordering (cart, sizes and flavours, branches, sign-in, checkout, tracking) is the page's own, unchanged.

## 1. What was built
- **Header:** the restaurant's logo and name in its colour, the **Delivery / Pickup switch** (only when the restaurant offers both — v1.95.0), search, Sign in (or the profile and My orders), and the cart with a count. On a phone it stacks: name and buttons, search, then the switch.
- **Hero:** leads with the restaurant's own **banner and tagline** (Settings → Online ordering) if it has uploaded one; otherwise **today's first active deal** with what is in it and an **Order now** button that adds it; otherwise the restaurant's name and tagline on its colour. Nothing is invented: no "today only" or discount the restaurant did not set. Hidden for dine-in QR orders.
- **Category tiles:** only categories with something to order, in the restaurant's order, with item counts; the category picture if it has one, otherwise an outline icon chosen from the name (pizza, burger, karahi, drinks, dessert, deals …) on a colour that stays the same for that name. A tile opens the category, which then shows a readable category rail.
- **Item cards:** picture (or the icon on the category colour), name, what is in a deal, price in the restaurant's colour ("From Rs …" for sizes), and one of Add (+), Choose size, or a quantity stepper. The Roman-Urdu text on the old cards ("Select karein") is gone.
- **Cart bar on phones:** "View cart" with the count and the total.
- The restaurant's own colour (Super Admin's customer-app theme) drives all of it, as before.

## 2. Found while doing this
Two defects in the first draft, caught in the browser and fixed before release: the category rail's text was nearly invisible (the old chips were made for a coloured header), and on a phone the stepper's "+" ran out of the card.

## 3. Not changed, and why
- This is **WEB-01 and the look of WEB-03/04** (categories and the listing). **Not yet built:** separate pages for a product (WEB-05), the sizes sheet (WEB-06), the cart as a page (WEB-07), checkout as the design's sections (WEB-08), phone sign-in screens (WEB-09), address and branch pages (WEB-10/11), offers, contact, order history as pages (WEB-15 – 18), and the closed/unavailable states (WEB-20). They exist today as the page's dialogs and drawers with their old look. Opening hours, extra banners and social links are not stored; the hero uses the one banner and tagline already in Settings.
- "Popular" items are not shown: the website cannot read order history, and nothing is invented.
