# QAT and UAT — DT POS Enterprise v1.84.0 (Super Admin console: reference layout and dashboard)

Requested by the owner with the "DT SALON ADMIN" screenshot: a brand sidebar, a dashboard of cards (total, waiting for approval, new phone requests, active, payment pending, expired, expiring in 7 days, suspended, blocked, collected this month, all salons' sales today, phones online now, outstanding), a waiting-for-approval list, renewals due with Expired / Active badges, and "+ New invoice". Invoice verification shipped in v1.83.0.

## 1. What was built
- **Brand sidebar** (large screens) with every console section; the Dashboard is first and is where the console opens. Small screens keep the existing pill strip. Every existing section is still reachable (pinned by a test).
- **Dashboard cards** (`lib/superAdminOverview.ts`): Restaurants · Waiting for approval · New device requests · Active (approved, plan not expired) · Payment pending (restaurants with an open invoice) · Expired · Expiring in 7 days · Blocked devices · Collected this month · Outstanding · Devices online now (seen in the last 5 minutes). Cards open the section behind them.
- **Waiting for approval** list (longest wait first) with **Approve** — the console's existing approve action.
- **Renewals due** (expired, or ending within 14 days; most overdue first) with **Expired / Expiring soon / Active** badges and an **Invoice** button that opens that restaurant's billing.
- **+ New invoice**: pick a restaurant, its billing opens.

## 2. Differences from the reference, on purpose
| Reference card | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| All salons' sales today | **not shown** | It lives in each restaurant's own data; the console does not read it. A number here would be invented. The page says so. |
| Suspended | merged into "Waiting for approval" | On Supabase a restaurant has one switch (`tenants.is_active`); there is no separate suspended state. |
| Blocked (salons) | Blocked **devices** | Devices can be blocked; restaurants cannot. |
| Phones online now | Devices online now | Same thing: every approved, unblocked device seen in the last 5 minutes. |
