- **v1.103.0** · 2026-11-05 · **Backup restore no longer hangs or leaves sync trouble** (owner report: "I upload a backup, sync trouble, the app sticks"). Found by reading the path, not guessing: (1) while a restore uploaded, every change it made woke the realtime reload, which saw each row the upload had not reached yet as "missing from the cloud" and queued it a second time, and the queue's flush then uploaded the same rows again at once; (2) the queue rewrote its whole contents to disk once per write-through op (quadratic: re-queueing 4,000 bills = 4,000 rewrites of a 4,000-row queue); (3) one network hiccup counted a whole chunk of 100 rows as failed; (4) pictures kept inside the data made single requests of many megabytes; (5) the page reloaded after a fixed six seconds, cutting a big restore off halfway, and nothing showed progress. Now: reloads and the queue's flush are held during the restore and reconciled once at the end; the queue keeps at most one write waiting; a failed chunk is retried (0.8 s, 2.4 s); requests are bounded in rows and bytes; rows that still fail are queued for the sync engine and listed; restored records are stamped as the newest word; the page shows a real progress dialog and reloads only when the upload is done (or says exactly what is left). **Colour scheme from the top bar** (DT Refined): a palette button switches between the nine schemes in one click, colours only. Not reproduced against the owner's own data (not available here); measured and tested on a 4,000-order backup  `005a394`
